![Axonometry Joglo Rooftop]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2021-11-08-joglo-rooftop/AXO.avif)

Urban housing is facing an affordability crisis everywhere. When affordable supply is short, entrepreneurs and developers usually fulfill the demand by using the economy of scale. People would pool together resources to be able to produce a lot of cheap things. But with wealth inequality also widening globally, there's less and less financial incentive in making affordable housing. Why struggle to make 10% return on an affordable project, when you can easily make more by selling fewer, more expensive units?

This project tried to address the problem by offering small scale, individual solutions. Unlike housing projects, these units don't require a lot of upfront investment. They provide the affordability of a large-scale project while still maintaining the flexibility of a small-scale solution.

The form of the unit takes inspiration from the roof of Joglo, a traditional Javanese typology. Besides the iconic form, the traditional roof form also provides climatic advantages. Its height and slanting sides allow for hot air to rise to the top, making room for cooler air at room level.

![Joglo Rooftop Climate]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2021-11-08-joglo-rooftop/joglo-clima-01.avif)

To reduce land cost, the units utilize existing roof area. Of course, this position exposes the housing units to more sunlight and heat. That's why bitumen roof tiles are used as the main exterior material, along with polycarbonate instead of glass. The materials would insulate the interior from excess heat. The stud construction also seeks to increase insulation by creating a layer of air barrier between the walls.

![Construction Sequence]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2021-11-08-joglo-rooftop/construction.webp)

But to keep the room comfortable in the hot, humid tropical climate, optimal air flow is the key. The door and window, covered with aluminum mosquito nets and positioned across each other, allow for cross ventilation 24/7. In addition, roof ventilation along the unit allows the rising hot air to escape.

![Axonometry Joglo Rooftop Climate]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2021-11-08-joglo-rooftop/AXO_CLIMA-01.avif)

Each housing unit provides enough space for standard bathroom appliances, desk, chair, and a folding bed. It provides similar amenities as the standard boarding house and hotels in Indonesia's urban areas.
