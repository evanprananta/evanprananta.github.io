"Dude, why are you so uptight?"

"Why am I so uptight, Jessica?" He laughed incredulously.
"I don't know. Maybe it's because suffering is an inherent fact of life. Or *injustice* — an irrepressible part of the *world*. Or maybe it's because the universe is hurtling towards entropy... Or maybe — just maybe — I was a little lonely as a child."
