I think there's a difference between *rendah hati* and *rendah diri*.

I certainly don't think myself perfect - I may have my insecurities, and I definitely have many flaws.

I may be a bit mushy around the outside, like an overly ripe banana. But unlike the banana, somewhere within me is a solid steel core of confidence.

And when the world is beating away at my being, 
when life is whipping me into nothingness - 
stripping me of everything I have 
and all that I am, 
I catch a glimpse of that core of steel,
and I know I'll be alright.
